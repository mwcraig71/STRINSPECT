---
name: Inspection assignment modes
description: Product convention for choosing field inspector responsibilities.
---

The primary assignment choice belongs in Inspection Settings: Topside + Underside is one combined field role, while Underwater is a separate role. Underwater is available only when South Carolina (SCDOT) is selected.

**Why:** Field teams typically divide work between inspectors who cover topside/underside work and dedicated underwater inspectors; a hidden header-only toggle was not discoverable.

**How to apply:** Keep the header control as a quick switch, but make Settings the authoritative, discoverable place to choose the assignment and update the corresponding photo checklist.
