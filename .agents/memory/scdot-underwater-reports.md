---
name: SCDOT underwater reports
description: Sample-backed conventions for recognizing and configuring South Carolina underwater bridge inspections.
---

Treat both `Underwater` and `Underwater (Unscheduled)` SCDOT report types as underwater inspections. Use four required general photos: upstream/upstation elevation, downstream/downstation elevation, view upstream from under the bridge or culvert, and view downstream from under the bridge or culvert.

**Why:** Three supplied SCDOT underwater reports consistently use this report classification and four-view photo pattern for both bridges and culverts.

**How to apply:** When importing an SCDOT report whose inspection type contains “Underwater,” select the underwater field mode and its agency-specific photo checklist while retaining normal SNBI element and condition-state import.
